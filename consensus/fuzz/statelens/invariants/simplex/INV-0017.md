---
id: INV-0017
title: Certification is abandoned only after a covering finalization
source_kind: spec
source_ref: consensus/src/simplex/mod.rs:103-109 (Certification)
scope: [replica, voter]
---

## Statement
If the replica has started certifying the notarized proposal of view `v`, then the replica shall
not abandon that certification (cancel, abort, or drop it without a result) unless it holds a
finalization for view `v` or a higher view.

## Rationale
A nullification of `v` can arrive while certification of `v` is still running. If the replica
abandons certification at that point, different honest groups, neither with a quorum, may refuse to
vote on each other's next proposals: one group built on the certified block, the other on the
nullification. Consensus then halts. Continuing certification until a finalization at the same or
a higher view makes the result irrelevant keeps the replica able to vote on the next honest
leader's block.

## Evidence
"Certification of some notarization should only be abandoned once a finalization at the same or
higher view is observed. Until then (say a nullification certificate for a view arrives before
certification completes), the application should continue attempting to complete certification.
... If we did not do this, it is possible that different parts of the network (neither with quorum)
would refuse to vote on each other's blocks (halting consensus)" (lines 103-109).

## Preconditions / assumptions
A crash-restart ends every in-flight request; that is not abandonment, and after the restart the
replica restarts certification for views that still need it. Certification that finishes with a
result (success or failure) is not abandonment. Pruning a view below a finalized view is covered by
the finalization.

## Observation hints
Certification starts from `State::certify_candidates` and `Round::try_certify`, and is aborted with
`Round::abort_certify` (`is_certify_aborted`) in `consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/round.rs`; the
voter actor holds the in-flight certify requests in `actors/voter/actor.rs`, and views are pruned in
`State::prune` and `Actor::prune_views`. The tests `test_certification_cancelled_on_finalization` and
`nullification_does_not_abort_inflight_certification` show the intended behavior. A check should
record the in-flight certifications and the highest finalization held in ghost state, and assert at
every abort, cancel or drop point.
