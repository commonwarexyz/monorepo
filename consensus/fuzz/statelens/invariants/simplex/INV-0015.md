## Statement
The replica shall not sign a notarize vote for a proposal `c` in view `v` with parent view `p`
unless all of the following hold: `p < v`; the parent is finalized, or notarized and certified by
the replica, or admitted under the optimistic rule below; the replica holds nullifications covering
every view strictly between `p` and `v`; and when `v` is not the first view of a term, `p = v-1`.
Under the optimistic rule, `p` is in the same term as `v`, at most `optimistic_views` views lie
between `v` and the last view the replica holds a notarization or finalization for, the replica's
own certification has not rejected the parent, and the replica has local evidence for the parent:
its own notarize vote for it, a notarization of it, or its explicit certification.

## Rationale
This is rule 1 of forced inclusion: a notarized proposal in view `v` must appear in the canonical
chain unless a nullification covers `v`. It holds only if honest replicas refuse to notarize a
proposal that skips a view without a covering nullification or builds on an uncertified parent. A
replica that notarizes such a proposal helps a Byzantine leader tail-fork around a notarized block.
The optimistic rule is the only exception, and its bounds (same term, issuance window, local
evidence) keep optimistic votes inert when an ancestor fails.

## Evidence
"If the container's parent `c_parent` is finalized (or both notarized and certified) at `v_parent`
and we have required nullifications covering the skipped views between `v_parent` and `v` (a
nullification covers the rest of its term; when `v` is not a term start, `v_parent` must be exactly
`v-1`), verify `c` and broadcast `notarize(c,v)`" (lines 40-45). "To propose in view `v+k`, the
leader must reference a certified parent in some view `v_p` and possess required nullifications
covering the skipped views from `v_p` to `v+k`" (lines 157-158). The optimistic conditions are
listed in lines 220-232.

## Preconditions / assumptions
A nullification of a view covers that view and the rest of its term. This applies equally to the
notarize vote a leader signs for its own proposal. Certificates count whether assembled, received,
fetched, or restored from the journal. A notarize vote replayed from the journal after a restart is
not a new signature.

## Observation hints
Notarize votes are signed in `State::construct_notarize` in
`consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/state.rs`. Parent checks live in `State::parent_payload`,
`validate_parent_span`, `ensure_parent_precedes`, `find_parent`, `explicit_parent_ready` and
`optimistic_parent_ready`; the optimistic window in `slide_optimistic_frontier` and
`has_failed_optimistic_ancestry`. Leader proposals go through `State::proposed`. A check should keep
ghost records of the certificates held and certification results per view, compute the condition
independently, and assert at each notarize signature.
