## Statement
While the replica has signed a nullify vote for a view `u` and has not observed a finalization of
a view at or above `u` in the same term as `u`, the replica shall not sign a finalize vote for any
view `v > u` in that term.

## Rationale
With stable leaders a nullification covers the view it was created for and the rest of its term,
so a later proposal can skip all of those views at once. That is safe only if no covered view is
finalized. The docs prove this with a quorum-intersection argument that rests on this gate: an
honest replica that voted `nullify(u)` withholds `finalize` for later views of the term until a
same-term finalization at or above `u` proves that the nullification can never form. A replica
that opens the gate early can help a finalization and a covering nullification form in the same
term, which lets a later leader fork around a finalized view.

## Evidence
"With stable leaders (`term_length > 1`), a prior same-term `nullify` vote blocks later `finalize`
votes until a covering finalization is observed" (Deviations from Simplex Consensus). "The finalize
gate maintains this invariant: a participant that voted `nullify(u)` withholds `finalize` votes for
later views in that term until it observes a same-term finalization at or above its highest
`nullify` vote" (Same-Term Vote Safety). "The gate also recovers from a `nullify` vote that never
became a nullification ... an observed same-term finalization at or above the vote proves the
nullification can never form, so the vote is inert and the gate reopens" (Same-Term Vote Safety).

## Preconditions / assumptions
Only meaningful when the term length is above 1; with term length 1 every term has one view and
INV-0001 covers the single-view case. "Observed a finalization" includes finalizations the replica
assembled, received, fetched, or restored from its journal. A nullify or finalize vote replayed
from the journal after a restart is the vote signed before the restart and still counts for the
gate. Rebroadcasting an already signed finalize vote is not a new signature.

## Observation hints
Finalize votes are signed in `State::construct_finalize` in
`consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/state.rs`, which consults `highest_local_nullify_in_term` and
`last_finalized`. Nullify votes are signed in `State::construct_nullify`. Term boundaries come from
the elector's `Terms` (`term_start`). A check should keep its own per-term ghost record of the
highest nullify vote signed and the highest finalization observed, rather than reuse the
implementation's bookkeeping, and assert at each finalize signature.

//! view `v` rules out a nullification at any view `u <= v` in the same term (otherwise a proposal
//! could fork around a finalized view).
//!
//! The finalize gate maintains this invariant: a participant that voted `nullify(u)` withholds
//! `finalize` votes for later views in that term until it observes a same-term finalization at or
//! above its highest `nullify` vote. To see why the invariant holds, suppose both a nullification
//! at `u` and a finalization at `v >= u` form in the same term. Their quorums intersect in at
//! least one honest participant that voted both `nullify(u)` and `finalize(c,v)`. If `u = v`,
//! this is impossible outright: no honest participant votes both `nullify` and `finalize` in a
//! single view. If `u < v`, the gate means that participant first observed a same-term
