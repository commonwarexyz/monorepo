## Statement

While a vote is retained for certificate assembly, when the replica processes another copy of
that vote, the replica shall discard the extra copy from consideration for that assembly.

## Rationale

A duplicate contributes no new participant to a certificate's quorum.
The issue discussion requires duplicate filtering during normal consensus processing and also
describes filtering during journal recovery.
This constrains vote admission before assembly, complementing the certificate requirements in
INV-0002 and INV-0003.

## Evidence

[Issue #2070](https://github.com/commonwarexyz/monorepo/issues/2070) reports a notarization
assembly attempt containing three votes but only two distinct signers.
An extra copy of one signer's vote reached assembly and triggered a panic instead of being
filtered out.
The maintainer [confirmed the duplicate-filtering requirement](
https://github.com/commonwarexyz/monorepo/issues/2070#issuecomment-3473238479).
[PR #2102](https://github.com/commonwarexyz/monorepo/pull/2102), merged in
[commit](https://github.com/commonwarexyz/monorepo/commit/6ca07aba2fee6a37c166885abc94724c77f0b679),
closed the issue by requiring unique committee credentials when configuring the committee.

## Preconditions / assumptions

The first copy remains retained on entry to this admission step, and the corresponding
certificate has not yet been assembled or accepted.
Copies have the same epoch, view, vote kind, signer, proposal if present, and signature.
The first copy may have originated locally or arrived over the network.
After recovery, the condition refers to votes actually restored or collected since restart.

## Observation hints

Observe the admission decision in `consensus/src/simplex/actors/batcher/round.rs:158` and
`consensus/src/simplex/actors/batcher/round.rs:225`, before independent verification,
certificate completion, or pruning.
Check that the additional copy creates no new contribution in the pending or verified votes
used by `consensus/src/simplex/actors/batcher/verifier.rs`; physical copies retained for
reporting are not additional contributions.
`consensus/src/simplex/actors/batcher/mod.rs:915` covers a local vote followed by its network
duplicate.
Journal replay currently avoids reseeding the batcher with old votes
(`consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/actor.rs:1069`).
