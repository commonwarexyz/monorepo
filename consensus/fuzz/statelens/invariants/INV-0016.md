## Statement
When the replica's certification of a proposal `c` has returned failure, the replica shall not
propose a container whose parent is `c` and shall not sign a notarize vote for a proposal whose
parent is `c`, unless it holds a finalization of `c` or of a descendant of `c`.

## Rationale
A failed certification means the application judged `c` unfit to extend. The protocol tolerates
`c` being notarized, but relies on honest replicas refusing to build on it, so that `c` is either
skipped by a nullification or never extended. A replica that loses the rejection (after a restart,
pruning, a replacement notarization, or an optimistic child verified before the result arrived)
would extend a chain the application rejected. A finalization overrides the local verdict because
it proves a quorum certified `c`.

## Evidence
"If `certify` returns `false`, the participant broadcasts `nullify` for the view instead (treating
it as an immediate timeout), and will refuse to build upon the proposal or notarize proposals that
build upon it" (lines 98-100). "Only move to view `v+1` and broadcast `finalize(c,v)` if
certification succeeds, otherwise broadcast `nullify(v)` and refuse to build upon `c`" (lines
128-129).

## Preconditions / assumptions
Certification is deterministic, so honest replicas agree on the result; this invariant concerns
only the replica's own result. A result restored from the journal after a restart counts. Votes
replayed from the journal after a restart are not new signatures.

## Observation hints
Certification results arrive through `State::certified(view, is_success)` in
`consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/state.rs`. Proposals are built after `State::proposed` and the
leader's propose path (`Round::propose_ready`); notarize votes are signed in
`State::construct_notarize`. The existing tests `failed_certification_blocks_optimistic_child_notarize`
and `replayed_failed_ancestor_blocks_direct_descendant_until_finalization` show the intended
behavior. A check should keep a ghost set of rejected proposals (view and digest) and assert at each
proposal and notarize signature.
