## Statement
The replica shall not assemble a notarization, nullification or finalization certificate for a
view unless it holds matching votes of that kind from at least a quorum of distinct participants.

## Rationale
Simplex safety depends on quorum intersection: any two quorums of distinct participants share at
least one honest participant. If repeated votes from one signer count toward the quorum, a
Byzantine participant that repeats its vote can stand in for missing honest votes. The replica
could then form a certificate that honest participants never supported, for example a
notarization or finalization that conflicts with another certificate for the same view.

## Evidence
Issue #2070: with quorum 3, the replica collected notarize votes [signer 3, signer 3, signer 4]
for one proposal in view 5, counted three votes, and started notarization assembly. Only two
distinct participants had voted. Assembly then panicked on the repeated signer, but the quorum
check before it had already counted the duplicate. The issue was closed by PR #2102 (merge commit
6ca07aba2fee6a37c166885abc94724c77f0b679).

## Preconditions / assumptions
Quorum is the committee's quorum size for the epoch of the view. Votes are "matching" when they
have the same kind and view and, for notarize and finalize, the same proposal.

## Observation hints
The quorum check before `try_construct_*` in the batcher verifier
(`actors/batcher/verifier.rs`). At the moment of assembly, compare the number of distinct signer
indices among the pending votes with the quorum. For attributable schemes, the finished
certificate's signer set has the same count.
