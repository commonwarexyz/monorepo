## Statement
While a floor it has accepted still awaits its anchor block, the replica shall not deliver
finalized blocks to the application.

## Rationale
An accepted floor replaces the replica's starting point: once its anchor arrives, delivery resumes
from the anchor, and the deliveries still awaiting acknowledgement are dropped. Delivering while
the anchor is outstanding would hand the application blocks from the sequence the floor replaces.

## Evidence
`consensus/src/marshal/core/floor.rs:93@55cd57fd2137`: repair and application dispatch must wait
for the floor anchor while a floor update awaits it.
`consensus/src/marshal/mod.rs:50-53@55cd57fd2137`: the floor is supplied as a finalization, and
marshal fetches the corresponding block asynchronously before dispatching application blocks above
it. The actor's dispatch resumes only after the floor anchor is durably stored. The test
`test_standard_pending_floor_drops_in_flight_ack_before_anchor` finalizes the next block while a
floor waits for its anchor and checks that it is not dispatched until the anchor arrives.

## Preconditions / assumptions
A floor is accepted when its finalization is valid and is for a later round than the replica's
round floor and than any floor whose anchor it already awaits; the floor configured as the
starting point is accepted the same way. The floor stops awaiting its anchor when the anchor block
arrives, whether it then advances the floor or turns out to be at or below the processed floor, or
when a later accepted floor replaces it. A stale or superseded floor therefore creates no lasting
obligation to wait.

## Observation hints
`State::blocks_progress` in `consensus/src/marshal/core/floor.rs` (`FloorState` in the actor) is
true while a floor awaits its anchor, and `Actor::try_dispatch_blocks`
(`consensus/src/marshal/core/actor.rs`) returns early on it. A floor is accepted in
`Actor::install_floor` (from `SetFloor` or the configured starting floor) and resolved in
`Actor::apply_pending_floor`. Check at the `Update::Block` report against a ghost flag set when a
floor is accepted and cleared when its anchor is applied. Whether a target reaches this depends on
its harness: the actor store target reaches `Mailbox::set_floor` through `StoreOp::SetFloor`, and
marshal's standard Twins targets can configure a starting floor (`Start::Floor`), so the
anchor-awaiting state is reachable in both; the other targets start at genesis.
