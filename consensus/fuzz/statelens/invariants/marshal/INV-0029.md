id: INV-0029
title: A block enters finalized history only with finalization evidence
source_kind: comment
source_ref: consensus/src/marshal/mod.rs:27
scope: [replica, core, resolver]

## Statement
When accepting a non-genesis block into its finalized history, the replica shall have a valid
finalization for that block, or for a descendant connected to it through authenticated parent
links.

## Rationale
Finalized history is what the replica delivers to the application and serves to peers as final.
A block admitted without a finalization, or through a parent link nobody authenticated, becomes
final for this replica and for every peer it backfills although consensus never finalized it, and
a block delivered to the application cannot be taken back.

## Evidence
`mod.rs:27` (Finalization): the actor only finalizes a block and its ancestors if it has a
corresponding finalization from consensus, and marshal concerns itself with verifying that a valid
finalization exists for the block. `resolver/handler.rs:315`: the commitment of a finalized-chain
fetch must be the payload of a verified finalization or the parent commitment of an archived
finalized block, and the finalized annotation (`resolver/handler.rs:214`) reuses only commitment
material authenticated that way. In the actor, a block fetched without certification evidence
(`resolver/handler.rs:197`) goes at most into the certified cache; only a delivery with a known
finalization or a finalized-chain annotation is stored as finalized.

## Preconditions / assumptions
A parent link is authenticated when the child is already in finalized history, or has a valid
finalization, and the accepted block matches the parent commitment the child names. A floor is
supplied as a finalization, so its anchor block falls under the first case. Genesis, and blocks
restored from the replica's own storage at startup, are not accepted here.

## Observation hints
Finalized history is the finalized-blocks archive and the finalizations-by-height archive in
`consensus/src/marshal/core/actor.rs`. Blocks enter it in `Actor::store_finalization` (a
finalization reported by consensus, or a resolver delivery handled in `Actor::handle_deliver` that
has a cached finalization or carries `Annotation::Finalized`), in `Actor::try_repair_gaps`
(walking down parent commitments from a finalized block), and in `Actor::apply_pending_floor` (a
floor anchor, whose finalization `Actor::install_floor` verified). `Actor::ensure_genesis_anchor`
writes genesis. Check where the block is put into the archive, against a ghost record of the
commitments whose finalizations the replica verified and of the parent commitments named by blocks
already in finalized history. Today's fuzz targets never install a floor.
