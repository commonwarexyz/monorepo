title: A block enters finalized history only with finalization evidence
source_kind: comment
source_ref: consensus/src/marshal/mod.rs:27
scope: [replica, core, resolver]

## Statement
When accepting a non-genesis block into its finalized history, the replica shall have a valid
finalization for that block, a block in its finalized history that names it as its parent, or its
own request for that block as part of its finalized chain.

## Rationale
Finalized history is what the replica delivers to the application and serves to peers as final.
A block admitted without a finalization, or through a link nobody authenticated, becomes final for
this replica and for every peer it backfills although consensus never finalized it, and a block
delivered to the application cannot be taken back. A finalized-chain request carries the evidence
the replica had when it made it, so the block it brings back needs no further evidence.

## Evidence
`mod.rs:27` (Finalization): the actor only finalizes a block and its ancestors if it has a
corresponding finalization from consensus, and marshal concerns itself with verifying that a valid
finalization exists for the block. `resolver/handler.rs:315`: the commitment of a finalized-chain
request must be the payload of a verified finalization or the parent commitment of an archived
finalized block, and the finalized annotation (`resolver/handler.rs:214`) reuses only commitment
material authenticated that way. In the actor, a block fetched without certification evidence
(`resolver/handler.rs:197`) goes at most into the certified cache; only a delivery with a known
finalization or a finalized-chain annotation is stored as finalized.

## Preconditions / assumptions
A parent link is authenticated when the child is already in finalized history, or has a valid
finalization, and the accepted block matches the parent commitment the child names. Finalizations
and finalized blocks count however the replica came to hold them, including those it restored from
its own storage at startup: they were valid when stored. A floor is supplied as a finalization, so
its anchor block falls under the first case. Genesis, and blocks restored from the replica's own
storage at startup, are not accepted here. Whether a finalized-chain request was made for an
authenticated commitment is a property of the request, not of this acceptance.

## Observation hints
Finalized history is the finalized-blocks archive and the finalizations-by-height archive in
`consensus/src/marshal/core/actor.rs`. Blocks enter it in `Actor::store_finalization`, whose
callers pass the block's finalization when they hold one (a consensus report, a verified finalized
delivery, or one cached or restored from storage), and in `Actor::apply_pending_floor` (a floor
anchor, whose finalization `Actor::install_floor` verified). `Actor::handle_deliver` stores a
resolver delivery as finalized when it has a cached finalization or carries
`Annotation::Finalized`, the marker of a finalized-chain request. `Actor::ensure_genesis_anchor`
writes genesis. At the write, accept a finalization passed with the block whose payload is the
block's commitment, a finalized-chain annotation on the delivery, and ghost records of
finalizations verified and parent links written. Ghost records alone are not enough: the restart
tests seed the archives directly (`seed_inconsistent_restart_state`, `seed_cache_block`), and
`test_coding_coalesced_block_delivery_trusts_finalized_subscriber` and
`test_standard_stale_floor_anchor_holds_dispatch_until_durable` enqueue finalized-chain deliveries
directly. Whether a fuzz target installs a floor depends on its harness: marshal's standard Twins
targets can start a node from a finalized floor (`MarshalTwinsInput.floor`, `Start::Floor`),
the actor store target installs floors at run time (`StoreOp::SetFloor`,
`Mailbox::set_floor`), and the other targets start at genesis.
