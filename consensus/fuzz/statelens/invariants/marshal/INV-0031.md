title: Finalized blocks are delivered in height order without gaps
source_kind: comment
source_ref: consensus/src/marshal/mod.rs:135
scope: [replica, core, application]

## Statement
When delivering a finalized block to the application, the replica shall deliver only a block whose
height is above its processed floor and is either the first height above that floor or one above
the height of the block it last delivered.

## Rationale
The application applies finalized blocks as a sequence: a skipped height leaves it without a block
it must apply, and a step back re-applies blocks it has already processed. Delivery is documented
as at least once, so a height may repeat, but a repeat restarts the sequence just above the
replica's processed floor.

## Evidence
`mod.rs:135`: finalized blocks are reported to the application in monotonically increasing order,
with no gaps permitted. `mod.rs:24`: the actor delivers a block at least once, the application
should be prepared for duplicate deliveries, and the blocks will be in order.
`core/mailbox.rs:961` (setting a floor): if the verified finalization advances the current floor,
marshal anchors on its block, prunes below it, then syncs and delivers blocks starting at the
floor height. The test `test_standard_pending_floor_drops_in_flight_ack_before_anchor` delivers
height 1, accepts a floor while that delivery awaits acknowledgement, and once the floor's anchor
arrives delivers height 1 again, then height 2.

## Preconditions / assumptions
While the replica has processed no block, every height is above its processed floor and the first
height above it is zero: genesis is delivered first. Installing a floor puts the processed floor
just below the floor's anchor, so delivery resumes at the anchor block. Repeats happen after a
restart and when a floor drops deliveries still awaiting acknowledgement. The Statement allows
them, and rules out a gap, a step back to an already processed height, and a repeat that does not
restart just above the processed floor. Finalized-tip notifications are not deliveries.

## Observation hints
Deliveries are the `Update::Block` reports in `Actor::try_dispatch_blocks`
(`consensus/src/marshal/core/actor.rs`). The next height comes from
`PendingAcks::next_dispatch_height` (`consensus/src/marshal/core/acks.rs`), seeded by the delivery
cursor in `consensus/src/marshal/core/stream.rs`, which is zero while nothing was processed and one
above the processed height afterwards. `Actor::apply_pending_floor` sets the processed height just
below an installed anchor, and `Actor::install_floor` and `Actor::apply_pending_floor` drop the
deliveries awaiting acknowledgement. Check at the report against the processed floor read at that
moment and the last delivered height kept by the actor. Whether a fuzz target installs a floor
depends on its harness: marshal's standard Twins targets can start a node from a finalized floor
(`MarshalTwinsInput.floor`, `Start::Floor`), the actor store target installs floors at run time
(`StoreOp::SetFloor`, `Mailbox::set_floor`), and the other targets start at genesis.
