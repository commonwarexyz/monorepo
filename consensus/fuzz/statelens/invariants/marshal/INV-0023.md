id: INV-0023
title: Inline notarization requires complete structural and application verification
source_kind: comment
source_ref: consensus/src/marshal/standard/inline.rs:5
scope: [replica, standard, application]
---

## Statement
When accepting a new proposal for notarization in inline mode, the replica shall have obtained
the block matching the proposed digest, checked its membership in the proposal's epoch and its
direct parent linkage at the next height to the parent named by the consensus context, and
completed application verification with a positive result under that context.

## Rationale
Inline certification relies on the honest members of the notarizing quorum having already
verified the block. Moving a positive verification response ahead of any of these checks would
invalidate that reliance and could admit an invalid block without later application validation.

## Evidence
`standard/inline.rs:8` permits a positive verify result only after parent/height checks and
application verification. `Inline::verify` applies the epoch precheck, awaits and validates the
parent, and then returns the application verdict. `standard/validation.rs:33` requires agreement
between the expected parent digest, the fetched parent's digest, and the child's parent field,
as well as contiguous heights.

## Preconditions / assumptions
The replica is honest. A new proposal has a digest different from its declared parent; valid
epoch-boundary re-proposals are covered separately by INV-0025. Inline blocks need not embed a
consensus context. Candidate storage may start before validation, and a positive verify result
does not itself promise that the candidate's durable sync has completed.

## Observation hints
Observe the positive response from `Inline::verify` in
`consensus/src/marshal/standard/inline.rs`, together with `await_and_validate_parent` and
`run_app_verify` in `consensus/src/marshal/standard/validation.rs`. Keep the consensus context
associated with the verification call; do not substitute a context reconstructed from the block.
