---
id: INV-0032
title: The processed floor advances only past fully acknowledged deliveries
source_kind: comment
source_ref: consensus/src/marshal/mod.rs:146
scope: [replica, core, application]
---

## Statement
When raising its processed floor other than by installing a floor, the replica shall already have,
for each height above the old processed floor up to the new one, a delivery of that height's block
whose acknowledgements have all been handled, made since it last started, installed a floor, or
started fetching a floor's anchor block.

## Rationale
The processed floor is the replica's durable record of how far the application has processed;
after a restart, delivery resumes just above it. Moving it past a block whose acknowledgements
were not all handled, or on the acknowledgement of a delivery the replica has since dropped, would
skip that block for good if the replica crashed, which breaks at-least-once delivery.

## Evidence
`mod.rs:146`: to ensure all blocks are delivered at least once, marshal waits to mark a block as
delivered until the application acknowledges the update. `mod.rs:151`: cloning the update shares
the block, and marshal only considers the block delivered once every acknowledgement is handled.
`core/actor.rs:1871`: acknowledgements are processed in FIFO order so the processed floor height
always advances sequentially. When a floor's anchor must be fetched, the actor drops the in-flight
acknowledgements before they can advance the processed height past the floor, and the test
`test_standard_pending_floor_drops_in_flight_ack_before_anchor` requires that an acknowledgement
released while the anchor is pending not count as progress.

## Preconditions / assumptions
Installing a floor sets the processed floor without deliveries, and restoring it from storage at
startup does not raise it. While the replica has processed no block, the heights above its
processed floor start at zero. A delivery's acknowledgements include those of every clone the
application made of the update. The acknowledgement of a delivery made before one of the points
the Statement names does not count, even if the application handles it later.

## Observation hints
Acknowledgements are awaited in FIFO order by `PendingAcks` (`consensus/src/marshal/core/acks.rs`)
and handled in `Actor::handle_ack` (`consensus/src/marshal/core/actor.rs`), which raises the
processed height through `Actor::update_processed_height`. `Actor::apply_pending_floor` also calls
it when installing a floor, which this invariant excludes. `Actor::install_floor` (before fetching
an anchor) and `Actor::apply_pending_floor` (when installing) drop the deliveries awaiting
acknowledgement into `cleared_acks`; a stale anchor that was already local drops none. An update's
acknowledgement completes only after every clone is acknowledged
(`commonware_utils::acknowledgement::Exact`). Keep the deliveries and the handling of their
acknowledgements in actor-owned ghost state, reset at the points the Statement names. Whether a fuzz
target installs a floor depends on its harness: marshal's standard Twins targets can start a node
from a finalized floor (`MarshalTwinsInput.floor`, `Start::Floor`), the actor store target installs
floors at run time (`StoreOp::SetFloor`, `Mailbox::set_floor`), and the other targets start at
genesis.
