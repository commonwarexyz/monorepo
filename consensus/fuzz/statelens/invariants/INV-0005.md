## Statement

When the replica processes repeated copies of a notarize, nullify or finalize vote, the replica
shall not abort consensus processing because the same vote was delivered more than once.

## Rationale

Repeated delivery is possible without a protocol fault, and a Byzantine participant can
repeat its own votes. Duplicate filtering is the replica's responsibility before certificate
assembly; a repeated vote cannot justify terminating the replica's consensus processing.

## Evidence

[Issue #2070](https://github.com/commonwarexyz/monorepo/issues/2070) reports two processes sharing
a signing key sending identical notarize votes for one proposal.
The receiving replica attempted certificate assembly with a repeated signer and panicked.
[PR #2102](https://github.com/commonwarexyz/monorepo/pull/2102), merged in
[commit](https://github.com/commonwarexyz/monorepo/commit/6ca07aba2fee6a37c166885abc94724c77f0b679),
closed the issue by enforcing unique committee identities and consensus keys.

## Preconditions / assumptions

The replica has a valid committee configuration. Copies refer to the same signed vote,
including its epoch, view, kind, signer, proposal if present, and signature.
The property applies before and after journal recovery; externally injected crashes,
shutdowns, and storage failures are separate events.

## Observation hints

Observe vote receipt in `consensus/src/simplex/actors/batcher/actor.rs:601`, duplicate handling
in `consensus/src/simplex/actors/batcher/round.rs:225`, and subsequent certificate construction.
Distinguish an input-induced actor termination from a crash explicitly imposed by the harness.
The low-level assembly API assumes unique inputs; exercise the replica's admission paths.
