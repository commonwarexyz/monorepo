## Statement
The replica shall not sign a finalize vote for a proposal `c` in view `v` unless all of the
following hold at that moment: the replica's own certification of `c` returned success; the parent
of `c` is explicitly certified or finalized; the replica has not observed the leader of view `v`
equivocate in view `v`; and `v` is above the highest view for which the replica holds a
finalization.

## Rationale
A finalize vote is the replica's promise that the proposal passed the application's certification
and extends certified ancestry. "A payload can only be finalized if a quorum of participants
certify it" holds only if honest replicas never vote finalize without their own successful
certification. Optimistic notarize votes may run ahead of the parent's certification, but finalize
votes must not, or a finalization could anchor a chain whose parent later fails certification.
After leader equivocation, a finalize vote could support one of two conflicting proposals. The
bound against the highest observed finalization is one step of the same-term safety proof.

## Evidence
"Attempt to certify `c` ... On success: enter `v+1` and broadcast `finalize(c,v)` (skipped if we
have broadcast `nullify(v)` or observed `l` equivocate in `v`)" (lines 50-54). "If `certify`
returns `true`, the participant broadcasts a `finalize` vote for the payload (unless it has
broadcast `nullify` or observed the leader equivocate)" (lines 97-98). "Only move to view `v+1` and
broadcast `finalize(c,v)` if certification succeeds" (lines 128-129). "Participants only vote
`finalize` for views above their highest observed finalization" (lines 194-195). "Certification
requests and `finalize` votes never run ahead: both require the parent's explicit certification
first" (lines 234-235).

## Preconditions / assumptions
A certification result restored from the journal after a restart counts as the replica's own
result. A parent that is finalized counts as explicitly certified; genesis (view 0) is implicitly
finalized. Equivocation means two conflicting proposals for view `v` attributed to its leader,
observed through votes or certificates. The nullify case is covered by INV-0001 and not repeated
here. A finalize vote replayed from the journal after a restart is not a new signature.

## Observation hints
`State::construct_finalize` in `consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/state.rs` checks
`last_finalized`, the same-term nullify gate and `explicit_parent_ready`, then calls
`Round::construct_finalize` in `actors/voter/round.rs`. Certification results arrive through
`State::certified(view, is_success)` and `Round::certified`; equivocation is tracked by the round's
proposal `Slot` (`has_unequivocated_proposal` in `actors/voter/slot.rs`). A check should record the
certification results and equivocations it has seen per view in ghost state and assert at each
finalize signature.
