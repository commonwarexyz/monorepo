## Statement
When the replica holds, for a view v at or above its current view, a finalization of view v, a
notarization of view v whose proposal it has certified, or a nullification of view v, the replica
shall enter the view that follows v: view v+1, or for a nullification the first view of the next
term (which is view v+1 when the term length is 1).

## Rationale
A view ends once it is decided: a finalization or a certified notarization shows that its proposal
extends the chain, and a nullification shows that a quorum agreed to skip it. An honest replica
that holds such a certificate but stays in view v stops taking part in the next view: it neither
votes on the next leader's proposal nor times out the next view, so the next view can stall with
fewer than a quorum of participating replicas. Entering the following view for every decided view
is also how a lagging replica catches up: a certificate for a later view moves it straight past the
views it missed.

## Evidence
Human-authored from the module documentation of `consensus/src/simplex/mod.rs`. "Upon receiving
`2f+1` `notarize(c,v)`: ... Attempt to certify `c` ... On success: enter `v+1`" (lines 48-53).
"Upon receiving `2f+1` `nullify(v)`: ... Enter `next_term_start(v)` (equivalent to `v+1` when
`term_length = 1`)" (lines 57-59). "As soon as `2f+1` nullifies or finalizes are observed for some
view `v`, the `Voter` will enter the corresponding successor view (`next_term_start(v)` for
nullification, `v+1` for finalization). Notarizations advance the view if-and-only-if the
application certifies them" (lines 81-83).

## Preconditions / assumptions
"Holds" covers valid certificates the replica assembled from votes, received from peers, fetched
through backfill, or restored from its journal after a restart. For a notarization, the replica
enters the following view only once certification of its proposal succeeds; if certification
fails, it votes nullify instead (INV-0012). The statement constrains what the replica does once it
holds a deciding certificate, not when such a certificate arrives: a replica that never obtains one
for its current view stays there until its timeouts lead to a nullification.

## Observation hints
The voter's current view is `State::current_view`, and view changes go through `State::enter_view`
in `consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/state.rs`. Certificates enter through
`State::add_finalization`, `State::add_nullification` and `State::add_notarization`, and
certification results through `State::certified(view, is_success)`. A check point is right after
each of these calls: if the replica now holds a deciding certificate for a view v at or above
`current_view`, then `current_view` must be past v (at `next_term_start(v)` for a nullification).
