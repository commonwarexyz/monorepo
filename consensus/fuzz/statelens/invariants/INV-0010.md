## Statement
While the replica holds no certificate of a kind for a view above its finalized view, when it has
received valid matching votes of that kind for the view from a quorum of distinct participants,
the replica shall assemble a certificate of that kind for the view from one vote per participant,
whatever repeated copies of those votes it also received.

## Rationale
A quorum of distinct participants that signed the same message is exactly what a certificate
attests, so once the replica holds one it has everything it needs to form the certificate and
advance. Repeated copies of a vote add no participant, so they must neither count toward the
quorum (INV-0003) nor stop the replica from forming the certificate once distinct participants
reach the quorum. Otherwise a Byzantine participant that repeats its vote could crash the replica
or leave it without the certificate, and the view could not progress.

## Evidence
Issue #2070 ("panic if there are several votes from one signer"): in a Twins-style test with
quorum 3, a replica collected notarize votes [signer 3, signer 3, signer 4] for one proposal in
view 5. It started notarization assembly and panicked with "duplicate signer index: 3". With
those inputs the correct outcome is no certificate yet. The replica should keep collecting and
assemble the notarization from one vote each of three distinct signers once a third distinct
participant's vote arrives. The maintainers stated that the batcher must skip duplicates during
normal operation, and that the voter dedups votes on journal replay. The issue was closed by
PR #2102 (merge commit 6ca07aba2fee6a37c166885abc94724c77f0b679).

## Preconditions / assumptions
"Matching" means the same epoch, view and kind, and for notarize and finalize votes the same
proposal. For notarize and finalize, the proposal is the one the replica tracks for the view: the
leader's proposal once the replica has seen the leader's notarize vote, or the proposal of a
verified certificate. Votes for another proposal do not count. The votes are for the replica's
current epoch and inside its vote admission window, so they were not pruned or skipped. "Valid"
means each signature verifies under the signer's consensus key. The committee satisfies INV-0004.
The replica has not crashed between receiving the votes and the check. After a restart, only
votes received or restored since recovery count.

## Observation hints
Votes enter through `Round::add_network` and `Round::accept_vote` in `actors/batcher/round.rs`.
Views are marked dirty, and `process_view` in `actors/batcher/actor.rs` calls `try_construct` at
the end of each batcher loop iteration for dirty views above the finalized view. A suitable check
point is after `process_view` returns for a view: if the distinct valid signers of matching
votes for the tracked proposal (or for the view, for nullify) reach the quorum, the round must
report `has_certificate(kind)`. Recovered certificates are forwarded with `voter.recovered`.
