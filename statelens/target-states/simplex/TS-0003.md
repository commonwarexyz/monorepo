## Statement
While every online replica has signed a nullify vote for view v and crashed before any
nullification of v formed, and the replica has restarted from its journal, the replica holds its
own nullify vote for v, restored from its journal, and is in view v.

## Rationale
After a crash of every online replica, the votes that could form the nullification of v exist
only in their journals, and with one participant silent every online replica's vote is needed for
a quorum. On replay the replica deliberately does not send its restored votes again and relies
on its timeout to recover. A replica that loses the restored vote, never sends it again, or signs
a finalize vote for v after the restart stalls the cluster at v or signs both votes for one view.
Reaching the state needs every online replica to crash in the window between its nullify vote
and the nullification, which random crash schedules rarely produce.

## Evidence
The test runs four participants; participant 0 never starts an engine and no links exist, so no
view can produce a certificate before the crash:
consensus/src/simplex/mod.rs:3260-3286@8a0d2cef732b. It waits until every online replica has a
nullify vote for view 1, waits once more so that every vote is durable, and crashes every online
replica before any nullification can circulate:
consensus/src/simplex/mod.rs:3362-3392@8a0d2cef732b. It then links the online replicas and
restarts each from its journal: consensus/src/simplex/mod.rs:3394-3410@8a0d2cef732b. The
restarted replicas must reconstruct the nullification of view 1, because every online vote is
needed for a quorum: consensus/src/simplex/mod.rs:3481-3493@8a0d2cef732b; that progress check
is the test's outcome and is dropped. On replay the voter reports its own restored nullify vote
and does not re-seed the batcher with it, recovering a cluster-wide shutdown by timing out:
consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/actor.rs:974-976@8a0d2cef732b and
consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/actor.rs:992-998@8a0d2cef732b. The restored state does not
depend on whether the links come back before or after the restart, so the History leaves the
links to the continuation.
