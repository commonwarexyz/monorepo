## Statement
While the replica has an outstanding request for the finalized block at a height h of epoch e
that it has not processed, the replica holds a peer's response to that request that carries the
block at h with a finalization of it that claims another epoch.

## Rationale
The replica decodes the certificate of such a response with the participant bounds of the epoch
that contains the requested height, so a finalization that claims another epoch was read under
the wrong bounds and must be rejected before any verification, and blamed on the peer. A replica
that accepted it, or verified it under the claimed epoch's participant set, could store a
finalization that no quorum of h's epoch formed; one that dropped its request on the rejection,
or blamed an honest peer, would leave the gap at h unrepaired. Honest peers always answer with
the finalization of h's own epoch, so a driver reaches this check only when a Byzantine peer
answers an outstanding request by height, which the existing drivers never script.

consensus/src/marshal/resolver/handler.rs:364-367@8a0d2cef732b), and a peer answers that
request. The delivery path takes the epoch of the requested height, decodes the finalization
with that epoch's bounds, and rejects a finalization that claims another epoch before it decodes

signatures are checked, so the state does not depend on who signed the finalization; the test's
own block, whose parent is not the cluster's genesis, is incidental, and the History uses the
block the cluster finalized at h.
