## Statement
While a certificate repair demand remains necessary and unsettled, the replica shall preserve a
pending or retryable request for its view, certificate kind, and purpose, without treating a valid
response admissible for the requested view that leaves the demand unmet as either completion or a
fault by its sender.

## Rationale
A notarization and a covering nullification answer different ancestry questions even when they
share a request key. Accepting either certificate as completion can strand a voter waiting for
the other. A certified construction floor can also advance while a proposal still needs evidence
below it. INV-0007 checks whether a certificate is valid, not whether it satisfies a particular
consumer's dependency; lost repair work can halt progress without producing an invalid vote.

## Evidence
"Proposal Ancestry" in `consensus/src/simplex/actors/resolver/mod.rs` distinguishes background
repair from proposal ancestry, requires blocked certification to query peers beyond the leader,
and specifies retry without penalty for valid but insufficient evidence. It also distinguishes
possession from successful certification. `State::settled` and `State::key_settled` in
`consensus/src/simplex/actors/resolver/state.rs` define settlement by certificate kind and explain
that retiring a shared key affects subscribers absent from the current delivery.
`Actor::apply_effects` in `consensus/src/simplex/actors/resolver/actor.rs` retires only background
work when the certified floor rises.

## Preconditions / assumptions
Demands belong to one epoch. A response is valid here only if its signature, epoch, and relationship
to the requested view pass the protocol's response-admission rules. Cryptographic validity alone
does not protect an unrelated response from rejection. Possessing the exact notarization settles
a notarization demand even while certification is pending; a recorded failed-certification verdict
also settles it because another copy cannot change the verdict. A nullification demand is settled
by a nullification covering its view. A finalization at or above a requested view settles its
demands. Raising the certified floor may retire background repair at or below that floor, but
cannot alone retire directed ancestry repair there.

Retiring a shared request key must not discard any other live demand, including a subscriber not
listed in the current delivery. When certification lacks its parent's notarization, its repair
must permit querying peers beyond the proposal's leader; merging that demand with a request
targeted at the leader must preserve the wider peer choice. A verification-only request may target
the elected leader. The invariant preserves repair capability, not a delivery deadline under
arbitrary network loss, and applies within a running incarnation.

## Observation hints
Record demands when the voter sends `Mailbox::resolve` and the resolver handles `Actor::resolve`
or `Actor::fetch`. Track each `Ask` by its view, `Kind`, and `Until` purpose, using independent
certificate and verdict history to decide settlement. Check `Actor::apply_effects` and
`Actor::retire` where retirement reaches the resolver engine, and `Actor::handle_resolver` where
delivery feedback exposes `Outcome::Complete`, `Outcome::Ignored`, or `Outcome::Ambiguous`.
A key-wide decision needs the outstanding subscription set, not just the delivery's `asks`. Follow
`State::certification_fetch` in the voter through the fetch's peer selection, including request
merging, to ensure the certification demand is not restricted to the leader. A request currently
being delivered or retried still represents pending work.
