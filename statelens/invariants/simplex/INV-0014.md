## Statement
The replica shall not sign a finalize vote for a proposal `c` in view `v` unless all of the
following hold at that moment: the replica's own certification of `c` returned success; when `v` is
not the first view of its term, or `c` is the replica's own term-start proposal that it voted for
early as the incoming leader of a pipelined handoff, the parent of `c` is explicitly certified or
finalized; the replica has not observed the leader of view `v`
equivocate in view `v`; and `v` is above the highest view for which the replica holds a
finalization.

## Rationale
A finalize vote is the replica's promise that the proposal passed the application's certification
and extends certified ancestry. "A payload can only be finalized if a quorum of participants
certify it" holds only if honest replicas never vote finalize without their own successful
certification. Optimistic notarize votes may run ahead of the parent's certification, but finalize
votes must not, or a finalization could anchor a chain whose parent later fails certification.
After leader equivocation, a finalize vote could support one of two conflicting proposals. The
bound against the highest observed finalization is one step of the same-term safety proof.

## Evidence
"Attempt to certify `c` ... On success: enter `v+1` and broadcast `finalize(c,v)` (skipped if we
have broadcast `nullify(v)` or observed `l` equivocate in `v`)" (Specification for View `v`). "If
`certify` returns `true`, the participant broadcasts a `finalize` vote for the payload (unless it
has broadcast `nullify` or observed the leader equivocate)" (Certification). "Only move to view
`v+1` and broadcast `finalize(c,v)` if certification succeeds" (Deviations from Simplex
Consensus). "Participants only vote `finalize` for views above their highest observed
finalization" (Same-Term Vote Safety). "Certification requests and `finalize` votes never run
ahead: both require the parent's explicit certification first" (Optimistic Validation).

## Preconditions / assumptions
A certification result restored from the journal after a restart counts as the replica's own
result. A parent that is finalized counts as explicitly certified; genesis (view 0) is implicitly
finalized. Equivocation means two conflicting proposals for view `v` attributed to its leader,
observed through votes or certificates. The nullify case is covered by INV-0001 and not repeated
here. A finalize vote replayed from the journal after a restart is not a new signature. Pipelined
handoffs (PR #5208) let a term's incoming leader relay its first proposal and cast its notarize vote
before the outgoing term's final view certifies, but never its finalize vote
(consensus/src/simplex/mod.rs:141-145@5ec909886846, consensus/src/simplex/mod.rs:245-246@5ec909886846):
the incoming leader's finalize vote waits for the parent's explicit certification
(consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/state.rs:787-795@5ec909886846,
consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/state.rs:1844-1865@5ec909886846). Any other term start, which is
every view under rotating leaders, has no parent requirement: a replica may certify and finalize it
before its own certification of the parent completes.

## Observation hints
`State::construct_finalize` in `consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/state.rs` checks
`last_finalized`, the same-term nullify gate and `explicit_parent_ready`, then calls
`Round::construct_finalize` in `actors/voter/round.rs`. Certification results arrive through
`State::certified(view, is_success)` and `Round::certified`; equivocation is tracked by the round's
proposal `Slot` (`has_unequivocated_proposal` in `actors/voter/slot.rs`). A check should record the
certification results and equivocations it has seen per view in ghost state and assert at each
finalize signature. The parent clause holds when the parent's round is explicitly certified or the
parent view is at or below `last_finalized`: a finalized parent's round may already be pruned, and a
replica that joined from a floor never had it, so `explicit_ancestry_payload(parent)` alone, which
reads only rounds still held, misses finalized parents and is not the clause. Derive the parent
the clause names from the replica's own records (the view's place in its term, its own notarize
vote and the round's leader for a handoff, its certification results and finalizations), not from
`required_certification_parent` or `explicit_parent_ready`, whose blind spots the check would
otherwise share.

    fn required_certification_parent(&self, proposal: &Proposal<D>) -> Option<View> {
        let view = proposal.view();
        self.previous_in_term(view).or_else(|| {
            let previous = view.previous()?;
            (proposal.parent == previous
                && self
                    .handoff_leader(view)
                    .is_some_and(|leader| self.is_me(leader))
                && self
                    .views
                    .get(&view)
                    .is_some_and(|round| round.broadcast_notarize()))
            .then_some(previous)
        })
    }

    /// Returns true when the proposal's required parent is explicitly
    /// certified (or finalized).
    fn explicit_parent_ready(&self, proposal: &Proposal<D>) -> bool {
        self.required_certification_parent(proposal)
            .is_none_or(|parent| self.explicit_ancestry_payload(parent).is_some())
    }
