id: INV-0047
title: Eligible notarizations are dispatched for certification
source_kind: comment
source_ref: consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/state.rs (State::certify_candidates)
scope: [replica, voter]
---

## Statement
When a retained, structurally admissible notarization above the finalized floor becomes eligible
for certification and has neither a prior certification attempt in the current incarnation nor a
restored certification result, the replica shall request its certification by the end of the next
completed pass that dispatches certification requests unless a covering finalization makes it
unnecessary first.

## Rationale
A notarization may initially wait for its parent to certify, then become eligible after the
replica has left its view or term. Losing that work or failing to wake it leaves the replica
unable to certify descendants even though all prerequisites are present. INV-0017 protects
requests after they start, and INV-0011 requires advancement after success; neither detects an
eligible request that never starts. Merely retaining an entry that is never scheduled does not
satisfy this obligation.

## Evidence
`State::wake_certification_child` and `State::certify_candidates` in
`consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/state.rs` wake a blocked child when its parent certifies or
finalizes and collect newly notarized or unblocked certification candidates. The candidate loop
in `Actor::run`, in `consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/actor.rs`, dispatches them before waiting
for the next event. "Certification" in `consensus/src/simplex/mod.rs` explains why nullification
must not abandon still-needed certification. The contract of `CertifiableAutomaton::certify`
in `consensus/src/lib.rs` makes a certification attempt single-shot, including channel closure,
while allowing another attempt after restart if no result was durably recorded.

## Preconditions / assumptions
Eligibility uses the prerequisites of INV-0045, including its term-start exemption; a malformed
proposal or one still blocked on its parent is not ready. The checkpoint is a completed dispatch
pass, with fair actor scheduling and application request dispatch able to complete, not an
unfinished execution prefix, shutdown, crash, or storage failure. Once eligible, the proposal
must not need an unrelated network message to be scheduled. Nullification or advancement to a
later view or term alone does not remove the obligation.

Outstanding requests and terminal attempts need no additional request in the same incarnation.
Terminal attempts include success, rejection, a closed response channel, and cancellation justified
by a covering finalization. A completed result restored from the journal also removes the need to
request certification. After recovery, a retained notarization with no restored result is assessed
again under the same eligibility rules; this complements INV-0017's existing recovery requirement.

## Observation hints
Maintain an independent set of held notarizations without an attempt or a restored result, and
record when their parent prerequisites become satisfied. Observe `State::add_notarization`,
`State::certified`, `State::add_finalization`, and replay. `State::wake_certification_child` and
`State::certify_candidates` manage the implementation's scheduling sets, which must not serve as
the sole oracle for which work is owed. Check after the candidate-dispatch loop in `Actor::run`
that each eligible obligation reached `CertifiableAutomaton::certify`, the application-visible
request site. Record dispatch before waiting for its result, so a legitimately pending or closed
response does not look like missing initial work. Check dependency wakeups even for views below
the current view and after a term skip.
