## Statement
The replica shall not sign two notarize votes for different proposals in the same view, and shall
not sign two finalize votes for different proposals in the same view, including across crashes
and restarts.

## Rationale
Every safety argument in the protocol counts each honest replica once per quorum: two quorums for
conflicting proposals of one view intersect in at least one honest replica, which is assumed to
have voted for only one of them. An honest replica that signs conflicting votes, for example after
a restart that loses its journaled vote or after a replacement proposal, behaves as a Byzantine
equivocator and breaks that argument, allowing conflicting notarizations or finalizations.

## Evidence
The view specification (Specification for View `v`) gives each replica one notarize vote per view
("broadcast `notarize(c,v)`") and one finalize vote per view for the certified proposal. The safety
proof relies on the quorum intersection containing an honest participant that "voted both"
(Same-Term Vote Safety), which only constrains honest behavior if an honest participant never signs
conflicting votes in one view. The implementation treats conflicting votes from one signer as
equivocation and blocks the signer.

## Preconditions / assumptions
Signing the same vote again (rebroadcast or journal replay) is not a second vote. Votes of
different kinds in one view are covered by INV-0001. The compromised Twins replica is excluded like
every other Byzantine replica.

## Observation hints
Votes are signed in `State::construct_notarize` and `State::construct_finalize` in
`consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/state.rs`, and restored from the journal through `State::replay`
and `Round::replay`. A check should keep a ghost map from view to the proposal of each vote kind the
replica signed and assert at each signature. StateLens ghost state is reset only for a fresh run,
not at a crash-restart inside a run, so the map also catches a conflicting vote signed after a
restart.
