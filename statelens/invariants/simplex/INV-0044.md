---
id: INV-0044
title: Verification verdicts apply only to their original context
source_kind: comment
source_ref: consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/state.rs (State::verification_is_stale)
scope: [replica, voter]
---

## Statement
When consuming an application verification completion, the replica shall use it to mark a proposal
verified, authorize a notarize vote, or trigger a verification-failure timeout only if the request's
epoch, view, full proposal, resolved parent digest, and process incarnation still match the context
to which the completion is being applied.

## Rationale
Verification approves or rejects a proposal in a particular ancestry context, not just a view or a
payload digest. A certificate can replace the proposal or its parent while verification is in
flight. Reusing the old success would approve unverified ancestry; reusing the old failure would
forfeit a view because of a proposal the replica no longer follows. INV-0015 checks whether ancestry
is admissible, but does not establish that it is the ancestry the application actually verified.

## Evidence
The comments on `State::verification_is_stale` in
`consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/state.rs` explain that a same-view certificate or a displaced
optimistic parent can make a completion stale; `State::verified` and `State::verification_failed`
discard stale successes and failures. In the same file, `State::verification_matches` compares
the complete saved proposal and resolved parent payload with the current context. The
`local_notarize_requires_application_acceptance` check in `check_fuzz_invariants`, in
`consensus/fuzz/simplex/src/invariants.rs`, deliberately accepts success under any parent digest
with the matching round, parent view, and payload, so it does not establish this binding.

## Preconditions / assumptions
The full proposal includes its epoch, view, parent view, and payload. A process incarnation runs
from startup or recovery until shutdown or crash. This condition concerns effects attributed to a
verification completion; an independent timeout or certificate may still change the same view.
Discarding a completion for a pruned or replaced context is allowed. A locally built proposal uses
its construction result, and replaying an already recorded vote does not consume a fresh
verification completion. A missing saved binding is not, by itself, proof that a result matches.

## Observation hints
Record the request and its context when `State::try_verify` and `Round::set_verifying` prepare
`Automaton::verify`. Follow the completion through `Actor::process_verified`, `State::verified`, and
`State::verification_failed` in the voter. Compare independent request history with the proposal
and parent in effect when verification status or timeout state is changed, rather than trusting
`State::verification_matches` as the oracle. If the verdict authorizes a vote, follow that
authorization through `State::construct_notarize` to `Actor::notify` and `Actor::publish_vote`,
where the vote reaches the batcher and peers. A stale completion may be ignored even when
another cause legitimately produces a vote during the same iteration.
