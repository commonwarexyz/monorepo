---
id: INV-0045
title: Certification starts only with a notarization and ready parent
source_kind: spec
source_ref: consensus/src/simplex/mod.rs (Optimistic Validation)
scope: [replica, voter]
---

## Statement
When requesting application certification of a proposal in view `v`, the replica shall already
hold a notarization of that exact proposal with a parent preceding `v` and, unless `v` starts a
term, have explicitly certified or finalized its parent at view `v-1`.

## Rationale
Optimistic voting allows a child to become notarized before its parent certifies, but does not
authorize certifying that child early. Certification is the application's commitment that a
payload is safe to finalize, so its prerequisite must hold when the application is asked to make
that commitment. INV-0014 checks finalize signing; a premature certification request can violate
the ordering even if no finalize vote follows.

## Evidence
In `consensus/src/simplex/mod.rs`, "Specification for View `v`" starts certification after
notarization, and "Optimistic Validation" requires explicit parent certification before
certification requests or finalize votes. The comments on `State::certification_parent_ready`
in `consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/state.rs` explain the term-start exemption: such proposals
cannot be issued optimistically, so their notarization already rests on explicit ancestry.
`Round::try_certify` in `consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/round.rs` requires a notarization and
checks that its complete proposal matches the certification candidate.

## Preconditions / assumptions
The notarization must be valid for the replica's epoch and match the proposal's round, parent
view, and payload. Certificates and successful certification results restored from the journal
count as held evidence. Finalization supplies an explicit parent anchor. A local notarize vote,
an indirect implication from a descendant, or pending parent certification is insufficient for a
non-term-start request. A term-start request still requires the matching notarization and an
earlier parent, but does not require local possession of the parent's certification or certificate.
Replaying a stored certification result is not a new application request.

## Observation hints
`State::certify_candidates` applies `State::certification_parent_ready` and then
`Round::try_certify`. In `Actor::run`, candidates become externally visible to the application at
the `CertifiableAutomaton::certify` call. Check there using independently recorded notarizations,
parent certification results, and finalizations, rather than only at candidate selection or
`State::construct_finalize`. The request contains a round and payload; retain its association with
the full notarized proposal so a matching payload under another parent cannot satisfy the check.
