title: Progress without finality cannot postpone escape deadlines

## Statement
While an advance, stall, or nullify-retry deadline remains applicable within one process
incarnation, the replica shall preserve that deadline across certificate arrivals, application
activity, timeout polling, and same-term view advancement, using an armed nullify-retry deadline
instead of an older stall deadline for the current view's next retry.

## Rationale
Receiving a notarization does not guarantee certification will finish, and certifying successive
views does not guarantee any view will finalize. Those events therefore cannot continually move
the deadlines that let the replica escape stalled progress. After nullify, resetting the retry
deadline on unrelated activity can suppress retries forever, while reusing an expired stall
deadline can turn retries into an immediate loop. INV-0012 checks the justification for a nullify
that occurs; it does not detect a deadline that disappears or moves indefinitely without a vote.

## Evidence
In `consensus/src/simplex/mod.rs`, "Specification for View `v`" keeps the advance timer armed
during certification and specifies nullify retries; "Deviations from Simplex Consensus" anchors
stall protection to the oldest entered unfinalized view of the current term. `State::next_timeout`
and `State::next_stall_timeout` in `consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/state.rs` give retry cadence
priority over an older stall deadline and select the oldest entered view above the finalized
floor. `Round::next_timeout` in `consensus/src/simplex/actors/voter/round.rs` arms each retry
deadline on the first timeout poll after nullify, including after replay, and retains it on
subsequent polls.

## Preconditions / assumptions
An entered current view's original advance deadline remains applicable while its certification
is incomplete, the view is undecided, and the replica has not voted nullify there. Receiving a
notarization or starting certification does not end this condition. An earlier permitted timeout
may cause nullify without changing the original advance deadline. Leaving the view ends its
round-local timeout obligation.

Stall protection applies only when configured. Its anchor is the oldest view the replica actually
entered in the current term that is above its highest finalized view, not an optimistic or skipped
view created only for bookkeeping. Certification or same-term view advancement does not move that
anchor's deadline. A covering finalization, departure from the term, or restart can change the
applicable anchor.

After a local nullify, the retry deadline is the time of the first subsequent timeout poll plus
the configured retry interval, not necessarily the publication time plus that interval. It remains
fixed until the retry or view exit; each retry permits arming one new interval. Restart creates a
new deadline schedule. These are deadline-preservation checks at completed local scheduling steps,
not a bound on network publication time during arbitrary scheduler or storage delays.

## Observation hints
Record entry times and configured timeout durations independently when `State::enter_view` arms
`Round::set_deadlines`, and record local nullify publication and subsequent timeout polls.
Track the oldest entered view above the finalized floor independently of `State::stall_anchor`.
Check deadline transitions in `Round::next_timeout`, `Round::latch_timeout`, and
`State::next_stall_timeout`, then check the effective deadline that `State::next_timeout` returns
to the waiters in `Actor::run`. An earlier legitimate trigger is allowed before nullify; an armed
retry must not be replaced by that old trigger or an expired stall deadline. Follow timeout
handling through `Actor::timeout` to `Actor::notify` and `Actor::publish_vote` to distinguish
preserved timer state from its eventual outward vote. Compare deadlines and their causes, not
only timeout reason labels.
