## Statement
When accepting a new proposal for notarization as an epoch participant in coding mode, the
replica shall have checked that the commitment's coding configuration matches the epoch's
participant set and its context digest matches the consensus context, and have established the
validity of the shard assigned to its own participant index under that commitment.

## Rationale
Distinct honest participants holding their assigned shards supply the availability needed for
reconstruction. Obtaining enough other participants' shards to reconstruct a block does not
establish this replica's assigned-shard obligation. Context and configuration checks bind that
obligation to the correct proposal and participant set.

## Evidence
`consensus/src/marshal/coding/marshaled.rs:904-906@55cd57fd2137` validates coding configuration and,
for new proposals, context digest. Its participant branch at
`consensus/src/marshal/coding/marshaled.rs:1060-1063@55cd57fd2137` explicitly waits for
assigned-shard verification and states that reconstruction from gossip alone is insufficient for
notarization. `consensus/src/marshal/coding/shards/engine.rs` keeps assigned-shard readiness
separate from reconstructed-block availability and cryptographically checks received shards
against their commitment.

## Preconditions / assumptions
The replica is honest and has a participant index in the proposal's epoch. Verifier-only nodes
have no assigned shard and are excluded. A new proposal differs from its declared parent;
valid epoch-boundary re-proposals follow the separate boundary-block path. Locally generated
shards from a correctly encoded proposal satisfy validity by construction. Full block
reconstruction, application verification, and durable storage need not precede ordinary verify.

## Observation hints
Observe `Marshaled::verify` and `subscribe_assigned_shard_verified` in
`consensus/src/marshal/coding/marshaled.rs` and `coding/shards/mailbox.rs`. In
`coding/shards/engine.rs`, distinguish `is_assigned_shard_ready` from `record.block()` and track
the epoch's own participant index. Successful reconstruction must not fabricate an assigned-shard
verification event.
