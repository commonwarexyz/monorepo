## Statement
When accepting a block reconstructed from shards in coding mode, the replica shall have
validated the reconstructed bytes against the requested coding root and established that the
decoded block digest, decoded coding configuration, and hash of the embedded block context
equal their respective components of the requested commitment.

## Rationale
A coding commitment binds more than the application's block digest. Reconstruction under a
coding root must not let different configuration or context data enter the cache under the same
requested commitment, where later consumers may rely on that association without reconstructing
or re-encoding the block again.

## Evidence
`try_reconstruct` in `consensus/src/marshal/coding/shards/engine.rs` reconstructs with the
commitment's configuration and root, decodes the block and configuration, and invokes
`validate_reconstruction` before caching a trusted coded block. `validate_reconstruction` in
`consensus/src/marshal/coding/validation.rs` rejects mismatched block digests, configurations, and
context hashes. The engine documents that successful coding decode has already checked the
encoded blob's integrity against the root.

## Preconditions / assumptions
The replica is honest and the coding scheme and hash satisfy their advertised integrity
contracts. This concerns accepting a reconstruction from network shards, not an incomplete
attempt or a trusted construction from locally built or certified data. Commitment consistency
alone does not establish application validity, correct ancestry, or durability.

## Observation hints
Observe `try_reconstruct` immediately before `cache_block` in
`consensus/src/marshal/coding/shards/engine.rs` and `validate_reconstruction` in
`consensus/src/marshal/coding/validation.rs`. Retain the originally requested commitment for
comparison rather than deriving the expected commitment from the accepted block.
