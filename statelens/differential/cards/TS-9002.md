## Statement
While the replica holds the height-1 candidate as verified and its parent is genesis, the
replica has verified and certified the candidate without issuing any fetch or registering a
local wait.

## Rationale
The first block's parent is the genesis block the replica started from, so verification and
certification must resolve locally. A regression that fetches the genesis parent, waits for
it locally or fetches the candidate it already holds shows in this state. The card's
witnesses are the absence stamps (no fetch, no wait, no targeted fetch) and the two wrapper
verdicts.

## Evidence
The source test persists the height-1 block as verified, then runs the real wrapper verify
and certify, both of which return true against the genesis parent, and asserts that no
fetch was issued (consensus/src/marshal/standard/mod.rs:2601-2650@392b116687). The scenario prefix
the differential test compares against is `StandardCertifyFirstBlockFetchesGenesisParent`
(consensus/fuzz/marshal/src/scenarios/scenarios.rs:291-364@392b116687).

        let block =
            B::<P>::new::<Sha256>(block_context.clone(), genesis_digest, Height::new(1), 100);
        let digest = block.digest();
        harness.verified(Node::B, 1, &block).await;
