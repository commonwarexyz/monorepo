## Statement
While the replica holds no block for a notarized candidate and a backfill answer carrying
that candidate is available, the replica has certified the candidate after issuing exactly
one round-bound notarized fetch, registering a local wait and issuing no targeted fetch.

## Rationale
Certification of a locally missing candidate must recover the block by its round, not by a
digest or height it cannot trust, and must not fan out targeted requests. A regression that
fetches by another key, fetches twice, skips the local wait or certifies without the
delivered block shows in this state. This is the differential test's reference card: its
events exercise the armed delivery, the round-bound fetch, the local wait and both reply
channels at once.

## Evidence
The source test arms the resolver response before `certify`, so the round-bound fetch resolves
through the injected notarization and block, then asserts the certification, the delivery
verdict, the local wait and the absence of targeted fetches
(consensus/src/marshal/standard/mod.rs:2915-2960@392b116687). The scenario prefix the
differential test compares against is
`StandardCertifyMissingCandidateFetchesByRound`
(consensus/fuzz/marshal/src/scenarios/scenarios.rs:193-262@392b116687).

## History
E1. harness: arms B's next backfill fetch with a notarization of the height-1 block d at
    view v on genesis, led by B and signed by a quorum, and the block itself.
    Check (B, v, d): the delivery is armed on B's resolver.
E2. harness: calls B's certify for d at v.
    Check (B as E1, v as E1, d as E1): B's certification of d at v is requested.
E3. B: issues one round-bound notarized fetch for v, registers a local wait for d and
    issues no targeted fetch.
    Check (B as E1, v as E1, d as E1): B's fetch history holds exactly one fetch, the
    notarized fetch for v, still active; B holds a local wait for d; B issued no targeted
    fetch.
E4. B: certifies d at v through the armed delivery.
    Holds (B as E1, v as E1, d as E1): B's certification of d at v returned true, the
    armed delivery for v was valid, the notarized fetch for v is still active, the local
    wait for d is registered and B issued no targeted fetch.

        // The missing candidate: a height-1 block at view 1 on genesis, led by
        // the source's `me` (Node::B under the participant permutation), built
        // as in the source.
        let genesis_digest = harness.genesis().digest();
        let round_one = round(1);
        let block_context = harness.context(round_one, Node::B, View::zero(), genesis_digest);
        let block = B::<P>::new::<Sha256>(block_context, genesis_digest, Height::new(1), 100);
        let digest = block.digest();

            harness.make_notarization(round_one, View::zero(), digest, &QUORUM_SIGNERS);

        // round-bound fetch resolves immediately.

            harness
