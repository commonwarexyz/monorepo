## Statement
While the replica's verification of a candidate it does not hold is parked on a local wait
and certification of that candidate arrives, the replica has issued exactly one round-bound
notarized fetch, received the candidate through it, and completed both the verification
and the certification.

## Rationale
Verification waits only for local delivery; certification must take the pending gate and
bump a round-bound fetch so a candidate a Byzantine leader withheld can still be recovered.
A regression that fetches at verification, fetches twice, or certifies without resolving
the parked verification shows in this state.

## Evidence
The source test calls verify for a missing block, arms the notarized response, calls
certify, and asserts that both verdicts are true and at least one round-bound notarized
fetch was issued (consensus/src/marshal/standard/mod.rs:3056-3110@392b116687). The scenario prefix
the differential test compares against is
`StandardCertifyBumpsNotarizedFetchForPendingVerify`
(consensus/fuzz/marshal/src/scenarios/scenarios.rs:562-633@392b116687), which strengthens the
fetch count to exactly one.

## History
E1. harness: calls B's verify for the height-1 block d at view v on genesis, led by B,
    which no node holds.
    Check (B, v, d): B's verification of d at v is requested.
E2. B: registers a local wait for d and issues no fetch; the verification stays pending.
    Check (B as E1, v as E1, d as E1): B holds a local wait for d, B's fetch history is
    empty and B's verification of d at v is pending.
E3. harness: arms B's next backfill fetch with a notarization of d at v and the block.
    Check (B as E1, v as E1, d as E1): the delivery is armed on B's resolver.
E4. harness: calls B's certify for d at v.
    Check (B as E1, v as E1, d as E1): B's certification of d at v is requested.
E5. B: bumps one round-bound notarized fetch for v, resolved by the armed delivery, and
    completes both the pending verification and the certification.
    Holds (B as E1, v as E1, d as E1): B's verification and certification of d at v both
    returned true, B's fetch history holds exactly one fetch, the notarized fetch for v,
    still active, and B issued no targeted fetch.
