  \note{This distinction is important. Appending does not promise durability. A successful sync does. If a sync is interrupted after I/O starts, its result is indeterminate in the usual storage sense, but recovery must still choose one complete epoch. The tests use the stronger arbitrary-subset model, not a prefix-of-writes assumption.}

  \vfill
  \takeaway{The decisive optimization is an API constraint: preserve old bytes and publish a new length.}

  \note{This is why CRC32C becomes sufficient once we narrow the primitive. It is not an authentication mechanism and it does not protect against someone deliberately editing the disk and checksum. The threat here is crash-torn local storage. For random overwrite, a CRC only tells us that data is bad; it does not provide rollback material.}

    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.49\textwidth}

      \vspace{0.08cm}
      \begin{tikzpicture}

      \end{tikzpicture}

      \vspace{0.08cm}
      \scriptsize At most \textbf{64 MiB aggregate} remains as an unresolved CRC tail for restart. Older bytes preflush before roots are staged: no rejection and no second payload write.
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}

  \vfill
  \takeaway{Descriptor bytes cap participants. Payload bytes do not: 64 MiB caps only restart CRC work. Contiguous uses at most 2 fixed or 4 variable participants.}

  \note{The Vec-based API is bounded in participant metadata, not payload bytes. The implementation rejects more than 32 dirty participants, but the replicated descriptor reaches the 4 KiB root-slot limit first: 28 is only the zero-name upper bound, and real partition names, blob names, and deletion records consume more space. This is checked before roots are staged. An epoch may contain arbitrarily much payload within ordinary storage and offset limits. As appends arrive, completed durability operations turn older immutable bytes into a trusted prefix. Publication leaves at most 64 MiB across the group under CRC32C, so restart work stays bounded without rejecting or copying a large epoch. Fixed contiguous rollover publishes at most prior and current roots; variable publishes the data/offsets pair for each, at most four roots.}
\end{frame}

  \note{Only a completed fdatasync-equivalent advances the trusted frontier F; sync_file_range or another writeback hint never does. Background preflush is coalesced while the producer continues, and it writes no root. At publication, each participant waits only for any durability frontier it must rely on. The descriptor names that trusted prefix and carries CRC32C for the remaining tail; tail budgets sum to at most 64 MiB across the group. The final participant barriers run concurrently. A very large append followed immediately by sync may still wait for a payload preflush and then the root barrier, but payload is never copied or written twice. Before root staging, a crash can expose only old. Once staging begins, the ordinary indeterminate window applies: old or complete new.}
\end{frame}

  \quad $\vert$ \quad new A / old B

  \quad $\vert$ \quad new A / new B\hfill\null

  \vspace{0.14cm}
  \eyebrow{When B has a temporal dependency on A}

  \vspace{0.08cm}
  \noindent\hfill
    \begin{tikzpicture}[node distance=.72cm]
      \node[box,text width=2.25cm] (pa) {publish A};
      \node[root,text width=3.0cm,right=of pa] (da) {A's decision is durable};
      \node[box,text width=2.25cm,right=of da] (pb) {then publish B};
      \draw[flow] (pa) -- (da);
      \draw[flow] (da) -- (pb);
      \node[right=.75cm of pb,font=\scriptsize,text=Accent,align=left] {old/old, new/old, new/new\\\textbf{never old A / new B}};
    \end{tikzpicture}
  \hfill\null

  \vfill
  \takeaway{Atomic membership is local. Put a temporal dependency in one group, wait at its edge, or add a higher-level WAL only when a total order is required.}

  \note{Removing the coordinator is both the performance win and a semantic boundary. Two disjoint start_apply calls have no shared sequence number and may cross their durable frontiers in either order. That locality is what avoids a global lock and a volume-like transaction layer interposed on the filesystem. Opening one participant repairs only its exact descriptor group; recovery does not scan or order unrelated groups. If B semantically depends on A, either include both mutations in one group or do not begin B until A's durable decision is proven. start_apply returning Ok proves durability; awaiting its handle additionally waits for activation and namespace cleanup. A workload that needs one total order across arbitrary groups still needs a higher-level root WAL or coordination layer.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{Recovery is correct for arbitrary surviving write subsets}
  \vspace{0.05cm}
  \scriptsize
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.6cm} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{4.55cm} X}
    \toprule
    \textbf{What validates after restart} & \textbf{What recovery can prove} & \textbf{Exposed result} \\
    \midrule
    No M/T proof; every exact P, descriptor, incarnation, candidate, and required suffix validates & The replicated descriptor names one complete group decision & New roots for the whole group \\
    No M/T proof; any required check is missing or mismatched & The exact group decision is incomplete & Old roots for the whole group \\
    Any exact materialized (M) or tombstone (T) root validates & The group crossed its durable frontier & Roll forward; repair peers and finish removal \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}

  \vspace{0.22cm}
  \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
    \begin{column}{0.32\textwidth}
      \eyebrow{Torn roots}\par\smallskip
      \scriptsize Root and witness checksums reject mixed spellings and partial installation. P is invisible to ordinary recovery.
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.32\textwidth}
      \eyebrow{Torn payload}\par\smallskip
      \scriptsize A trusted prefix plus CRC32C over the remaining bounded suffix prevents partial visibility.
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.32\textwidth}
      \eyebrow{Threat boundary}\par\smallskip
      \scriptsize CRC32C detects crash damage on trusted local disk; it is not tamper authentication.
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}

  \vfill
  \takeaway{The protocol never assumes that surviving writes form a prefix in submission order.}

  \note{This is the central correctness argument. Unsynchronized writes can survive in any combination. The old roots remain self-contained. A new group is selected only from exact, checksummed evidence replicated in the named participants, or from a later materialized/tombstone proof that makes roll-forward mandatory.}
\end{frame}

    \node[panel,text width=3.6cm,minimum height=.76cm,right=.42cm of caller,align=center] (read) {long-lived read integrity\\\scriptsize record / page lifetime};
    \coordinate (fork) at (bytes.south |- caller.west);

    \draw[flow,draw=Amber] (caller) -- (read);
    \node[font=\scriptsize,text=Fault,above,yshift=.02cm] at ($(fork)!0.55!(caller.west)$) {hash again};
  \end{tikzpicture}

  \vspace{0.18cm}
  \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
    \begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
      \eyebrow{A possible bridge}
      \scriptsize
      \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{0.03cm}
        \item Accept caller-supplied spans; return composable \texttt{(offset, len, CRC32C)} receipts.
        \item Combine CRCs across write/preflush chunks without rereading bytes.
        \item Caller maps receipts through its existing index and persists them in the same UNO group.
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
      \eyebrow{Why this is not automatic}
      \scriptsize
      \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{0.03cm}
        \item A write may contain many records; one record may cross writes or preflush ranges.
        \item Byte offsets alone do not identify application items; variable journals need their offsets index.
        \item Root tags cannot hold a checksum map; storage-owned boundaries would recreate metadata.
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}

  \vfill
  \takeaway{Promising direction: share checksum computation, while callers retain ownership of logical boundaries.}

  \note{UNO currently needs only one CRC over the not-yet-proven-durable suffix. That checksum answers whether a candidate epoch is complete after a crash; it is not a permanent record index or a promise to validate every later read. CRC32C values can be combined when lengths and order are known, so a receipt API might avoid hashing the same bytes twice. The difficult part is boundary mapping: append calls, preflush ranges, pages, and logical records do not necessarily align. A useful experiment would let contiguous provide a boundary plan and consume receipts while keeping codecs and indexes out of the blob layer.}
\end{frame}
